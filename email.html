This address is separate from your Pack username. Only you can set it for your own account.

Changing your address disables old email notifications and reply links until you verify and turn them on again.

2. Choose your preferences

Emails may be stored by your mail provider and appear on your lock screen. Do not forward reply-enabled emails. Disappearing messages, group messages, calls and feed alerts are not emailed in this release.

How to reply

Open a notification email, tap Reply and write REPLY: your message on the first line. Send it normally. Reply addresses are single-use and expire after 24 hours. Replies are plain text only.

Web push stays independent. Email notifications are checked approximately once per minute, are skipped for already-read messages, and capped at 30 queued messages per account per day. Delivery depends on your email provider.

Administrator setup

Email delivery requires a Resend API key, verified sender domain, inbound address and signed webhook. Configure these as Supabase Edge Function secrets; never paste keys into GitHub or this page.
