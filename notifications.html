1. Phone backup · ntfy

Recommended when Samsung Internet says a notification was sent but nothing appears. ntfy is a separate app. No email, domain, API key or payment is needed for the free public service.

Android appiPhone / setup guide

Public ntfy topics are not password-protected. The generated random topic is a secret capability: anyone who obtains it could read or send generic alerts. Do not share it, show it in screenshots or reuse it elsewhere. Only event categories are sent, not names, conversations, photos or GPS. ntfy receives the topic and event category. Its public service has limits and no delivery guarantee.

Copy your code, then subscribe to it inside ntfy.

In ntfy, tap +, enter the code in Topic, keep the server set to ntfy.sh, then subscribe. Allow Android notifications for ntfy itself.

Silent, disappearing and already-read direct messages are excluded. Muted groups and Do Not Disturb are respected when the worker checks a job, except for security sign-in alerts and manual tests. Plan alerts cover personal and directly shared events, meetings, tasks and countdowns; they are checked each minute. Sign-in alerts cover new sessions, not failed login attempts or refreshes. Free safety limits: 35 queued alerts per account and 180 across The Pack per 24 hours; provider limits may be lower. Tests count. The backup sends alongside web push, so duplicates are possible. ntfy cache is disabled; offline devices may miss alerts. Turning off the topic stops future alerts but cannot recall a notification already sent.
