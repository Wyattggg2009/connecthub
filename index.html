    Headset & Google voice

    Off by default.

    When the browser routes media buttons to The Pack, play/pause can open voice commands and pause or skip can cancel an active assistant. Buttons never confirm a send or call. Long-press voice assistant buttons belong to your phone. Alt + Shift + V opens voice commands on a keyboard. Google speech recognition/voices depend on your browser and device; no Google Voice telephone account is linked.

    Custom sound library

    Up to 20 seconds and 2 MB each, 20 sounds per account on this device. Supported formats depend on your browser. Sounds stay on this device.

    Preferences are saved per account on this device. Automatic reading respects your preferences and can continue while a supported browser keeps this page running. Alerts outside the app use your device's notification sound. Messages may be spoken where other people can hear them.

    📍 Location sharing

    Choose who can see your location. Sharing is off until you start it. Only selected, permitted contacts can view the location. Updates require The Pack to be visible; closing or locking the phone pauses GPS. Last known positions are shown for up to 24 hours after a fix.

    Pack map

    Choose an incoming share to view it on the map. Loading a map sends the selected location to OpenStreetMap. No map loads automatically.

    Stopping sharing revokes access in The Pack; it cannot remove copies or screenshots a recipient already saved. Opening a map sends the chosen coordinates to Google Maps.

    Voice assistant

    Only an explicit yes confirms a send or call. The microphone listens for one response at a time; cancel stops it. Your browser may process speech using an online recognition service.
